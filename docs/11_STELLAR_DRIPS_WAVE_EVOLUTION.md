# Hut4Devs — Stellar & Drips Wave Evolution Study

> Turning everyday collaboration into trails of trust built by us and for us-all.

## Status

This is a living architecture and learning document.

It records how Hut4Devs evolves from its original accommodation and peer-support concept into a broader open-source community infrastructure project while studying and adopting Stellar where blockchain provides genuine value.

Development will begin on Stellar Testnet before any Mainnet consideration.

---

# 1. Product Evolution Since the Original Hut4Devs README

## 1.1 Original Direction

Hut4Devs began from a real accommodation coordination problem involving co-located fellows.

The initial product direction focused strongly on:

- accommodation payments;
- accommodation accountability;
- payment evidence;
- accommodation administration;
- reducing repeated forms and screenshots;
- peer-to-peer support.

The project has since expanded substantially.

---

## 1.2 Current Product Identity

Hut4Devs is now understood as:

> A community infrastructure for belonging, accountability, support, and trust.

Its relationship model is:

> Belonging tells us who we are together.  
> Accountability tells us what we owe one another.  
> Support tells us how we help one another.  
> Trust grows from how we repeatedly handle those relationships.

Core operating cycle:

**Coordinate → Support → Account → Repair → Grow**

Accommodation remains the first real-world implementation domain, but it is not the boundary of the product.

---

## 1.3 Institution-Aware Architecture

Hut4Devs is not designed only for Learn2Earn.

An institution may configure its own context while remaining part of the Hut4Devs platform.

Examples include:

- Learn2Earn
- Semicolon
- Decagon
- GoMyCode
- future participating institutions

Institution configuration may eventually include:

- institution name;
- institution logo;
- paired Hut4Devs + institution branding;
- local member terminology;
- campuses;
- accommodation properties;
- accommodation benchmarks;
- delegated responsibilities;
- governance configuration;
- document branding;
- institution-specific UI presentation.

The underlying Hut4Devs product remains shared.

---

## 1.4 Institution-Specific User Experience

Each participating institution may have a recognizable UI presentation without requiring a separate product implementation.

The institution's identity can be applied as configuration rather than duplicated application code.

Hut4Devs therefore distinguishes between:

**shared product capability**

and

**institution-specific presentation and configuration**.

---

## 1.5 Branded Accommodation Documentation

Accommodation records should eventually support downloadable documents.

A generated accommodation document may pair:

**Institution Logo × Hut4Devs Logo**

and include appropriate accommodation information such as:

- member identity;
- institution;
- campus;
- property;
- room;
- accommodation period;
- commitment amount;
- relevant verification information;
- document identifier;
- generation timestamp.

Private accommodation information must not automatically become public blockchain data.

The possibility of recording a cryptographic fingerprint of a document on Stellar will be studied separately from storing the actual document.

---

## 1.6 Neutral Member Identity

The universal platform identity is **Member**.

Institution-specific terminology is configuration.

Examples:

- Learn2Earn → Fellow
- another institution → its configured member terminology

Responsibilities such as Room Captain or Accommodation Coordinator are delegated capacities, not separate human identities.

---

## 1.7 Governance Principle

Hut4Devs distinguishes:

**Identity → Capability → Responsibility → Authority**

Technical capability does not automatically confer legitimate authority.

A person may have the ability to perform an operation without having the governance authority to perform it in a particular context.

---

## 1.8 Shared Room Model

The shared room is one domain object viewed through different scopes.

A Member, Room Captain and Accommodation Coordinator should not see three contradictory realities of the same room.

Instead:

> The Member visits their room.  
> The Captain operates within their room.  
> The Coordinator oversees rooms.  
> They are all looking at the same room.

---

## 1.9 Peer Support

Hut4Devs peer support includes distinct relationship types:

### Gift
No repayment obligation.

### Lend / Borrow
Repayment expected and may occur partially or fully.

### Contribute
Shared support toward a collective goal without an investment return.

A debt may explicitly be forgiven and converted into a gift.

A gift cannot later be converted into debt.

---

## 1.10 Trust Trails

Hut4Devs does not use a universal social credit score.

Trust should emerge from factual, contextual history rather than a single permanent human ranking.

The platform therefore favors append-oriented trails describing what occurred, who had authority, and how responsibilities were handled.

---

## 1.11 Recognition

Recognition should describe demonstrated behavior rather than create permanent status classes.

Core principle:

> We noticed what you repeatedly demonstrated here.

---

## 1.12 Fix a Puzzle

Community members can surface missing product or community puzzle pieces.

The flow supports:

- logging the problem;
- choosing how the reporter wishes to participate;
- preserving the original report;
- updating participation without creating duplicate reports;
- community visibility where appropriate.

---

## 1.13 Current Visual System

Hut4Devs now uses the Warm Daylight visual system.

The interface prioritizes:

- light warm surfaces;
- strong readable typography;
- warm borders;
- restrained caramel accents;
- clear hierarchy;
- accessible interaction states;
- preservation of semantic status colors;
- a separate dark-mode system.

---

## 1.14 Open-Source Direction

Hut4Devs is being developed as an open-source project.

The next stage of the project will intentionally develop:

- transparent contributor pathways;
- repository governance;
- scoped authority;
- technical ownership boundaries;
- meaningful issues;
- PR review culture;
- contributor education;
- interview-ready engineering experience.

---

## 1.15 Stellar Direction

Hut4Devs intends to use Stellar as its blockchain ecosystem where blockchain genuinely improves the product.

The project will begin with **Stellar Testnet**.

No Mainnet deployment should occur merely for demonstration purposes.

Mainnet use must follow sufficient:

- architectural understanding;
- testing;
- security review;
- threat modeling;
- key-management planning;
- operational readiness.

The project will study:

- Stellar accounts;
- wallets;
- assets;
- payments;
- transaction signing;
- Stellar SDKs;
- RPC;
- Soroban smart contracts;
- Rust;
- programmable authorization;
- cryptographic verification;
- appropriate on-chain and off-chain boundaries.

---

## 1.16 Repository Direction

Hut4Devs intends to move toward multiple independently meaningful implementation repositories.

Initial target:

1. Hut4Devs Frontend
2. Hut4Devs Backend
3. Hut4Devs Stellar / Blockchain

Repository separation is an architectural decision intended to create clearer technical and governance boundaries.

It must not be used to manufacture artificial work or artificial contribution opportunities.

---

## 1.17 Learning Principle

The project will evolve while its contributors learn.

New blockchain concepts will not automatically become product requirements.

The expected sequence is:

**Learn → Understand → Question → Relate to Hut4Devs → Decide → Document → Implement → Test → Review**

Core principle:

> Understand before automating. Validate before scaling. Solve the human problem before choosing technology.

And:

> Always fall in love with the problem.
